# Cardle

**Cardle** is a daily car guessing game inspired by Wordle. Each day, a new car model is selected, and you have 10 attempts to guess it correctly. Think you know your cars? Test your knowledge now!

[Play Cardle Here](https://play-cardle.com/)

---

## How to Play

1. **Guess the Car**: Enter your guess for the car model.
2. **Receive Feedback**: After each guess, you'll receive hints to guide your next attempt.
3. **10 Attempts**: You have up to 10 tries to guess the correct car model.
4. **New Challenge Daily**: A new car is featured every day, so come back for fresh challenges!

---

## Features

- **Extensive Car Database**: Includes a wide range of car models to challenge your knowledge.
- **Interactive Interface**: User-friendly design for an engaging experience.
- **Daily Updates**: A new car model is featured each day to keep the game fresh.
